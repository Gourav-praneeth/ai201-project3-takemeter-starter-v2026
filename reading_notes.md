# Reading notes — Milestone 1

**Community:** r/boardgames (public subreddit, board-game discussion). Chosen because
it has no spoilers, plenty of text, and a wide range from one-line quips to long
arguments. Source: the `boardgames` subset of
[HuggingFaceGECLM/REDDIT_comments](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceGECLM/REDDIT_comments),
sampled in [data/boardgames_pool_200.csv](data/boardgames_pool_200.csv).

**What I read:** the 40 comments in [data/reading_40.txt](data/reading_40.txt)
(numbers below are the `#` in that file). These are comments, not top-level posts.
They were sampled across four length bands, so short and long ones are both present.

## Candidate distinctions

1. **Some comments rest on a detail you could check; most rest on the poster's
   say-so.** #28 names a prior game ("Vast: The Crystal Caverns came out two
   years ago") and quotes the article it disputes. #31 names GMT's P500 and says
   customers "aren't even charged until the last minute." By contrast #7 ("An IQ
   is such a tiny insignificant part of a whole person") and #38 ("The theme and
   design is timeless") give reasons, but none of them could be shown wrong.

2. **Some comments tell what happened to the poster; others say what is true.**
   #35 (friends playing Illuminati with a mismatched die), #23 (first purchase was
   Dominion or Ticket to Ride) and #29 (playing Scrabble at 7) narrate and stop.
   #36 ("The negative reviews actually helped me enjoy this game") uses a personal
   experience to deliver a verdict.

3. **Some comments are there to get or give practical information, not to take a
   side.** #16 is a for-sale list. #25 is a recommendation request in a fixed
   template. #3 ("the Bring n Buy Auction at Breakout will probably be your best
   bet") and #37 (which cards changed in which expansion) answer a question
   without arguing anything.

4. **Length does not track substance.** #38 is 153 words of reasons for a feeling.
   #31 is 49 words and is more checkable. #2 is 65 words that sound like analysis
   (designer margins, "less than minimum wage") but give no number.

## Posts that sat between two ideas

- #38: a long list of concrete features (Portuguese tiles, zero setup, clack of
  cubes) whose conclusion ("timeless") is still just a judgement.
- #2: a general claim about designer pay with reasoning but no figure or source.
- #15: a story from play that ends in a claim about how people target players.
