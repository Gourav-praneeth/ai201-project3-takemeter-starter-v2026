With training any AI you need to give it some guidance otherwise you're wasting a tremendous amount of power having it learn a whole bunch of things that aren't useful towards make it better.

With a board game, whether it's victory points or some kind of achieve victory condition first it doesn't matter. You can simulate thousands of games per minute, so the AI is going to learn quickly what actions lead to more victory. After each generation, the best neural network is taken and used as the baseline for the following generation. 

Depending on the complexity of the system you are testing, it may only take few thousand generations to approach something resembling a new player, and maybe only a hundred thousand generations to become a master.
